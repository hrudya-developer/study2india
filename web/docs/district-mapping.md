# District mapping audit

Generated by `scripts/district-report.ts` (normaliser v1). Official names: data/districts.ts, Census of India 2011, updated for later official creations and renames (LGD unreachable), retrieved 2026-10-24. Matching code: lib/districts.ts.

How a value is mapped, in order: the official name after ignoring case, spacing, punctuation and a leading or trailing "District"/"Dist."; the hand-checked alias list (misspellings and renames); the hand-checked list of towns that lie wholly inside one district; for compound values ("Nitte, Udupi", "Prayagraj (Allahabad)") each part, when all parts agree. Values that are split districts, places across several districts, districts of another state or unknown are not mapped, so they stay out of the district filter.

Each institution takes the first value that maps: the district from each council list that names it (AICTE, DCI, INC, NCH, NCISM, PCI, VCI, in that order), then its city, then the councils' city values. A metro city given only as the city (Hyderabad, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru, Delhi) is not mapped, because colleges given that city lie in several districts.

Match types:

- `exact`: official spelling
- `normalised`: same name once case, spacing, punctuation and "District" are ignored
- `alias`: misspelling or former name, checked by hand
- `town`: town inside one district, checked by hand
- `part`: compound value; its parts agree on one district
- `ambiguous`: split district or place across several districts: not mapped
- `city-spans-districts`: metro city whose colleges lie in several districts: not mapped as a city
- `other-state`: a district of another state: not mapped
- `unknown`: not a district or known town: not mapped

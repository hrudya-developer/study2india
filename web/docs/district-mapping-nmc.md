# District mapping audit: NMC and NCH colleges

Generated by `scripts/district-report-nmc.ts` (normaliser v2). Matching code: `placeFromCollegeName` and `placeFromAddress` in lib/districts.ts; every place they find goes through `matchDistrict`, so only official district names, hand-checked aliases and hand-checked towns in data/districts.ts are ever placed.

## Sources

- **NMC lists of colleges teaching MBBS and PG courses** (data/nmc/mbbs.json, data/nmc/pg.json). The rows as retrieved carry the college code, name, state, university, management, year and seats: no address, city or district field. The official pages on nmc.org.in could not be reached from the build environment (the proxy refused the host), so they were not re-checked for an address column. NMC's names often end with the town ("Government Medical College, Ernakulam"), so the town is read from the name.
- **NCH college permission status** (data/councils/nch.json): an address line for every college, no district or town column. The district and town are read from the address.
- **NIRF rankings** (data/nirf): a city for every row, already placed through the normaliser. The NIRF-only institutions still without a district are all in metro cities (see below).

## Method

A name is read in the order written: the words after the college's own name, the parts after its commas, then bracketed former names; a value marked "Dist."/"District" anywhere counts too. The first place named decides: when it is a metro city or a split district ("Akbarpur, Kanpur": Akbarpur is the seat of both Ambedkar Nagar and Kanpur Dehat) nothing is placed, otherwise every place found must agree on one district, and a disagreement places nothing. Roads named after a town ("Thuraiyur Road") are skipped. Only when the name gives no place at all does a town it starts with count.

An address is read from the end: the district marked "Dist."/"District", and the town in the last part once the PIN and state are dropped (earlier parts only while the later ones are not places, and only the part just before a marked district). A marked district and the town must agree.

The town found fills an empty city; a metro city that cannot be placed in one district still fills the city. The loaders write both on insert and fill them only where empty on update; the boot-time normaliser (planDistricts) applies the same reading to databases loaded earlier, after the council district columns, the institution's city and the councils' city columns.

- `segment`: a part after the college's own name ("…, Ernakulam"), or the town at the end of the address
- `trailing`: the words after the name's last "College"/"Hospital"/"Institute" ("Nandha Medical College & Hospital Erode")
- `district-mark`: a value marked as the district ("Dist. Jalna", "Villupuram District")
- `leading`: the town the name starts with, used only when no other part is a place ("Coimbatore Medical College")

## Not placed

Why a name or address placed no district:

- `city-spans-districts`: metro city whose colleges lie in several districts (Bengaluru, Chennai, Hyderabad, Kolkata, Delhi)
- `ambiguous`: split district or place across several districts (Mumbai, Navi Mumbai, Burdwan, Midnapore, Imphal, Guwahati, Akbarpur)
- `conflict`: the text names places in two different districts (often a town and its pre-split district)
- `unknown`: no word read is an official district or a hand-checked alias or town (often the name carries only a founder's or trust's name)
- `other-state`: the place named is a district of another state
- `none`: the text names no place at all

## Caveats

- Names and addresses that give a district as it was before a split are placed in the district of that name: Andhra Pradesh's 2022 reorganisation (a college named "…, Chittoor" or "Krishna Dist." may now lie in Tirupati or NTR), and Telangana addresses giving "Medak" for mandals now in Siddipet or Sangareddy. Where the same text also names a town in the newer district, the two disagree and nothing is placed.
- Metro cities are not placed from a name or address alone: the colleges named "…, Bangalore" include ones in Bengaluru Rural (Hoskote, Nelamangala) as well as Bengaluru Urban, and those named "…, Chennai" include ones in Chengalpattu, Tiruvallur and Kancheepuram. Their district needs each college's own address, which none of these lists gives.

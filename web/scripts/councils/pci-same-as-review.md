# PCI same_as review

Decisions on which Pharmacy Council of India (PCI) records in `data/councils/pci.json` are the same institution as a record already loaded from AICTE, INC, NMC, DCI or NIRF. Linked codes are listed in `pci-same-as.json`, which `pci-convert.mjs` turns into each row's `same_as`. When the seed runs, the PCI record is loaded onto (or merged into) that record instead of getting its own page.

## How the candidates were found

From the local `study2india` database (read only), every PCI record that has no other identifier was compared with every record carrying an `aicte`, `inc`, `nmc`, `dci` or `nirf` identifier in the same state. Names were normalised (lower case, `&` to `and`, punctuation dropped, common abbreviations expanded: Govt, Instt, Coll, Sci, Univ, Shri/Sri; small words `of`, `and`, `for`, `in`, `at`, `the` ignored) and compared on the name up to the first comma, the whole name, and the name without a trailing bracket. PCI names that contain a university were also compared with that university's own record. This gave 307 PCI records with at least one candidate. The 125 NIRF Pharmacy-ranked institutions were then checked by name against PCI records in the same state.

## Rules

- Link only when it is the same institution: the same name at the same place (town, district or PIN from PCI's address or name, against the target's city, district, address or name), or the PCI entry is the pharmacy college or department of that exact university or institution at the same place.
- Generic names (Government Polytechnic, Government Polytechnic for Women, Government Pharmacy College, School of Pharmacy, Institute of Pharmacy, Institute of Medical Sciences) are linked only with a matching town, or a matching district where it is the only record of that name in the district.
- When PCI gives no address, a distinctive name is linked only when it is the only institution of that name in the state on both the PCI list and the other list.
- A university record is the target only when it is the university itself; a different faculty of the same university (for example a School of Engineering record) is not.
- One record takes at most one PCI unit per course: when a university has two PCI units with the same course, only its own department is linked, because loading both onto one record would overwrite one intake with the other.
- Different town or district, different campus, two possible targets, or two PCI units that could be the target: not linked.
- Where several records of the same name sit at the same place, the link goes to the one with the most data (a NIRF identifier and rankings first, then the most courses). `same_as` points at one identifier, so the other records stay; they are listed under duplicates below.

## Summary

- New links: 226 (aicte 182, nirf 9, nmc 4, inc 31). With the 90 earlier NIRF links, `pci-same-as.json` now holds 316.
- Rejected: 90.
